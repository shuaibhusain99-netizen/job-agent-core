Pick a job

Its ad, why it matched and your application tools show up here.

Settings

Everything here is stored in the data folder next to the app, on this PC only.

Where to search

Search looks for jobs in a country. "Prepare applications" also writes a cover letter and answers for each match there; with it off, matches show in Worth a look.

Minimum match to land in To apply (30–90)

Your details in letters

Used in cover letters, e-mails and application forms. Only on this PC - never sent to the AI reviewer.

New letters use them; letters already written keep the old ones.

Daily search

Each search asks the sites only for ads posted since the last search, so a daily search takes a few minutes. Once a week, or after you change what to search, it does a full search of all recent ads (about 10 minutes). If the PC is off or asleep at that time, it runs as soon as the PC is on.

Program version

Updates install in place: the same JobAgent.exe, with the same list and settings - no downloading by hand. Your list and settings are also backed up by themselves (the last 20 copies), and put back if the list ever goes missing.

AI reviewer off

Reads each promising ad the way a recruiter would - real requirements, nationality limits, the level of the role - and writes a one-line verdict. It can also write a cover letter for one ad. Free with a Groq account (no card), or fully private with Ollama on this PC. It never receives your name, phone or e-mail.

Get one at console.groq.com, under API Keys.

E-mail applications

Ads that say "send your CV to …" get a Send application button. It sends from your own address with your CV attached and keeps a copy in the sent folder.

Not your normal password. For Gmail: turn on 2-Step Verification, then create one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. Stored encrypted for your Windows account. Changing the provider or address asks for it again.

Job boards read in your browser

These sites block programs, so the agent opens their search pages in its own Edge/Chrome window on this computer and reads the job lists, like you browsing. It never clicks, signs in or applies. If a site asks to check you're human, it's skipped for that search and tried again next time.

Job sources

LinkedIn, SEEK, Rigzone, Gulf Job Paper and about 45 employers' own career sites need nothing.

Optional: Jooble keys for extra listings

Each Jooble country site needs its own free key from that site's /api/about page. The global jooble.org key mainly covers Canada. Saved keys are never shown again - paste a new one to replace it.
