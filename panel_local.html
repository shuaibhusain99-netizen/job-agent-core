Daily search

A full search takes about 30 minutes in the background - each site is asked slowly so it doesn't block you. The daily search runs at 07:00 if this computer is on.

AI reviewer off

Reads each promising ad the way a recruiter would - real requirements, nationality limits, the level of the role - and writes a one-line verdict. It can also write a cover letter for one ad. Free with a Groq account (no card), or fully private with Ollama on this PC. It never receives your name, phone or e-mail.

Get one at console.groq.com, under API Keys.

E-mail applications

Ads that say "send your CV to …" get a Send application button. It sends from your own address with your CV attached and keeps a copy in the sent folder.

Not your normal password - create an app password in your mail account's security settings. Stored encrypted for your Windows account.

Job sources

LinkedIn, SEEK, Rigzone, Gulf Job Paper and the employers' own career sites need nothing. Jooble adds listings from other job boards; each country site needs its own free key from that site's /api/about page. The global jooble.org key mainly covers Canada.

Job boards read in your browser

These sites block programs, so the agent opens their search pages in its own Edge/Chrome window on this computer and reads the job lists, like you browsing. It never clicks, signs in or applies. If a site asks to check you're human, it's skipped for that search and tried again next time. No e-mails needed.
