Agent

A full search takes about 30 minutes in the background (each site is asked slowly so it doesn't block you). You can keep using the app meanwhile. The daily search runs at 07:00 if this computer is on.

Countries

Search = look for jobs there · Apply = write an application pack for each match.

Matching

Below the minimum a job goes to Review instead of To apply. 55 balanced · 65 strict · 45 wide.

E-mail applications

Apply on site opens the job in a separate browser window (Edge/Chrome with its own profile — sign in to LinkedIn, Bayt etc. once and it stays signed in). On the job's own site and known job boards it fills the fields you can see from your profile; on any other site it waits until you press Fill this page. Your CV is attached only when you press Attach CV. It never ticks consent boxes, never answers personal or legal declarations, and never submits — you check the form and press the site's own button. The JobAgent bar on the page also has I submitted ✓.

Ads that say "send your CV to …" get a Send application button. The e-mail goes from your own address with your CV attached; a copy is kept in data/sent.

Not your normal password: create an app password in your mail account's security settings (Gmail: Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords). It is stored only in data/mail.json on this PC.

Job sources free keys

LinkedIn, SEEK, Rigzone, Gulf Job Paper (newspaper ads) and employer sites need no key. Jooble adds Bayt, GulfTalent and Naukrigulf listings for each Gulf country — one free key per country from xx.jooble.org/api/about.

Where are keys stored?

In data/keys.json next to the app, saved with the Save button.
